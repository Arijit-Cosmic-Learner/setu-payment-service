📖 How It Works: The Payment Lifecycle

Every transaction travels through deterministic states. Events can arrive asynchronously or out-of-order without corrupting ledger integrity.

1
payment_initiated

1. Checkout Initiated

The customer initiates payment on the merchant app. Transaction record is created with initial state.

2
payment_processed

2. Gateway Processed

Bank/Payment gateway authorizes and captures the amount. Transaction state updates to processed.

3
settled

3. Merchant Settlement

Funds are successfully credited to the merchant account. Reconciliation marks transaction as completed.

✕
payment_failed

Failure Path

If the bank declines or times out, the failure state is recorded for automated refund auditing.
